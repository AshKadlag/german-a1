## What is this?

A self-contained, no-install web app for German A1 exam preparation. Every word comes with **English AND Marathi (मराठी) translations** — making it one of the very few German study tools designed specifically for Marathi-speaking students in India or abroad.

- ✅ **300 essential A1 words** across 22 topic categories
- ✅ Marathi (मराठी) + English translations for every word
- ✅ Flashcard practice with flip animations
- ✅ Hören (listening) exam practice with audio playback
- ✅ Sprechen (speaking) exam simulation cards
- ✅ Schreiben (writing) — formal & informal letter templates
- ✅ Works offline after first load — no app to install
- ✅ 100% free, no login required

## App features

### 📚 Vocabulary
Browse all 300 words by category. Each card shows the German word, its English translation, and the Marathi translation (मराठी). Flip cards to test yourself.

### 🎯 Exam Practice
- **Lesen** — reading comprehension exercises
- **Hören** — listening exercises with built-in audio (uses your device's text-to-speech, no external service needed)
- **Schreiben** — writing exercises

### ✍️ Schreiben
Formal and informal letter-writing templates for the A1 Schreiben exam part. Covers the standard German letter format with common phrases in German and English.

### 🗣️ Sprechen
60+ speaking practice cards simulating the A1 oral exam — introducing yourself, asking questions, and responding.
